🌄 Red Rocks survival kit

  • 🚌The plan: Red Rocks Shuttle from the flagpole outside Union Station (by The Crawford Hotel, 1701 Wynkoop St). Leaves 45 min before doors, so about 5:15 PM. $65 round trip, back 30 min after the music ends from the Upper North Lot. Full refund if you cancel 24 h+ ahead. Book both nights once the dates are listed.
  • 🎫Backup 1: AXS "Shuttles to Red Rocks" add-on (On Location). At other AEG shows it's $70 from Thirsty Lion at Union Station (1605 Wynkoop St), check-in opens 1½ h before doors, all sales final, photo ID. Not listed for these two shows yet.
  • 🚍Backup 2: Bus to Show, a nonprofit that runs to Red Rocks shows. Rides post on bustoshow.org. After the show its buses wait in Lower South Lot 2.
  • ⛰️Altitude: Red Rocks sits at 6,450 ft (Denver is 5,280). Drink lots of water and take the stairs slow. You can bring an empty reusable bottle up to 32 oz.
  • 🎒Bag: one-pocket bag up to 13 × 15 × 8 in, or a fanny pack up to 6 × 9 in. Multi-pocket bags get turned away.
  • 📷Camera: fixed-lens cameras are fine. No removable-lens cameras, GoPros or selfie sticks.
  • 🚫Also banned: glitter, flow toys, umbrellas, gum.
  • 🧤Weather: shows go on in wind and snow. Layers, warm hat, gloves, a poncho and shoes for the uphill walk. Text RRWEATHER to 67283 for day-of alerts.

🚆 Getting around (no taxis)

  • 🚇DC Metro: Silver Line + Blue Line to Reagan National on the way out. Dulles is on the Silver Line, so the ride home is one train.
  • 🏔️Denver A Line DEN ⇄ Union Station: ~37 min, every 15 min, every 30 min in the evening. $10 Airport Day Pass covers every RTD bus and train for the rest of that day (RTD has floated raising it). Buy in the MyRide app or tap your card or phone on the validator.
  • 🚌16th Street FreeRide: free bus every 4–12 min between Union Station and Wade Blank Civic Center Station, ~15 min end to end, early morning to late night. RTD's 2027 budget options included cutting it by mid-2027, so check it's still running before you go.
  • 🚈Denver light rail (Meow Wolf day): E or W Line from Union Station one stop to Empower Field at Mile High. $5.50 Day Pass in MyRide ($2.75 for 3 hours).
  • 🤠Dallas DART: Orange Line DFW Airport (Terminal A) ⇄ West End, ~50 min. Red/Blue one stop West End ⇄ Union Station, which connects underground to the Hyatt. Green Line to Deep Ellum. $6 Day Pass or $3 for 3 hours in the GoPass app. Skylink between DFW terminals is inside security.
  • 🐻TBRU shuttles (2026 times): from the Hyatt from 7 PM Thu–Fri and 7:30 PM Sat, last bus back 2:30 AM, plus a 3 AM bus after BearDance. Pickup: follow the "Trinity Crossing" signs to the outdoor patio.
