🗺️ Route ideas for days 1–6

🏰 Disney + Paris → London

Disneyland Paris · Paris · Eurostar → London
  • A Disney park day to kick things off
  • Paris for photos, food and the Marais at night
  • Eurostar goes straight into London St Pancras, so it's train-only, no extra flight

🚲 Amsterdam → Paris → London

Amsterdam · Paris · Eurostar → London
  • Canals and architecture for the camera
  • Two big nightlife cities before the tournament
  • All by train once you land

🪩 Berlin → Amsterdam → London

Berlin · Amsterdam · → London
  • The most party-heavy option: Berlin's club scene, then Amsterdam
  • Longer hops: Berlin → Amsterdam is a long train day or a short flight
