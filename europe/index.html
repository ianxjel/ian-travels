🪩 Berghain notes

  • Klubnacht runs Sat night → Mon morning. Plan on Sunday Feb 14 (try ~8–11 AM)
  • Door only for Klubnacht, no advance tickets. Bring cash (~€20–30)
  • Lines can run 3–6 h. Go solo or with 1–2 people, wear dark simple clothes, no selfies in line
  • No photos inside (camera sticker). Shoot the building outside first
  • Friedrichshain, short walk from Berlin Ostbahnhof
