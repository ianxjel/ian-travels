🌄 Show-night survival kit

  • 🚌No car, no taxi: Red Rocks Shuttle from the flagpole outside Union Station (by the Crawford Hotel, 1701 Wynkoop St). Leaves 45 min before doors, so about 5:15 PM. $65 round trip, back 30 min after the music ends from the Upper North Lot. Full refund if you cancel 24 h+ ahead.
  • 🚍Cheaper backup: Bus to Show, a nonprofit that says it runs to every Red Rocks show. Rides post on bustoshow.org. After the show its buses wait in Lower South Lot 2.
  • ⛰️Altitude: Red Rocks sits at 6,450 ft (Denver is 5,280). Drink lots of water and take the stairs slow. You can bring an empty reusable bottle up to 32 oz.
  • 🎒Bag: one-pocket bag up to 13 × 15 × 8 in, or a fanny pack up to 6 × 9 in. Multi-pocket bags get turned away.
  • 📷Camera: fixed-lens cameras are fine. No removable-lens cameras, GoPros or selfie sticks.
  • 🚫Also banned: glitter, flow toys, umbrellas, gum.
  • 🧤Weather: shows go on in wind and snow. Layers, warm hat, gloves, a poncho and shoes for the uphill walk. Text RRWEATHER to 67283 for day-of alerts.
